## Real specimen scans shown through Sketchfab embeds

The "Real specimen" viewer embeds 3D scans of bones from the osteological collection of the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine, University of León (Spain), published on Sketchfab by **PaleoLab-ULE**: https://sketchfab.com/PaleoLab-ULE

The scans are displayed through Sketchfab's official embed viewer and are not downloaded, modified or redistributed by this project. All rights remain with their owners.
